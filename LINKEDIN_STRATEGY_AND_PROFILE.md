## 1. Anonymity & Account Setup Blueprint

### The LinkedIn Identity Dilemma
LinkedIn strictly requires personal profiles to reflect real legal identities and occasionally demands government ID verification (via Persona or CLEAR). If a personal profile is flagged as fake or a pseudonym, it risks suspension.

To maintain 100% anonymity while projecting maximum credibility to startup founders and hardware engineering leads, use the **Hybrid Agency Model**:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               YOUR PRIVATE IDENTITY (Hidden Admin)                 |
|             (Real account, never visible to public)               |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|             LINKEDIN COMPANY PAGE (Primary Brand Entity)          |
|    e.g., "SpectraIQ Imaging" / "ChromaTune Labs"                   |
|    - 100% Anonymous: LinkedIn never reveals page admins.          |
|    - Lists Services, Case Studies, Technical Insights, Reviews.   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  ^
                                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|          ANONYMOUS CONSULTANT PROFILE (Front-Facing Persona)       |
|    e.g., "Alex V. - Principal ISP Tuning & Imaging Consultant"    |
|    - Listed as Founder / Lead Consultant at Company Page.         |
|    - Used for direct outreach, networking, and direct messaging.  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Operational Security (OpSec) Checklist
1. **Domain & Email**:
   - Register a dedicated domain (e.g., `spectratune-imaging.com` or `luminaisp.com`) via Cloudflare Registrar or Namecheap with **WHOIS Privacy** enabled.
   - Create a Google Workspace or ProtonMail professional address: `alex@spectratune-imaging.com` or `inquiries@spectratune-imaging.com`.
2. **Phone Number**:
   - Use a dedicated physical prepaid SIM or reputable eSIM/business VoIP (e.g., OpenPhone) that can receive LinkedIn SMS verification.
3. **Browser Isolation**:
   - Use a separate browser profile (Chrome/Brave profile) dedicated exclusively to this business identity.
4. **Profile Picture Options**:
   - **Do not use**: Celebrity faces, cartoon avatars, or generic stock photos that appear on reverse image search.
   - **Option A (Professional AI Headshot)**: Use tools like Aragon.ai or Midjourney to generate a realistic, professional headshot in an optics/tech lab setting.
   - **Option B (Brand Monogram / Studio Emblem)**: Use a sleek, minimalist geometric camera aperture / prism logo. (Best for Company Page; for a personal profile, a realistic AI persona builds significantly higher DM response rates).
5. **Booking & Calls**:
   - Set up an anonymous Calendly link configured with your business domain email.
   - For discovery calls, you can join Google Meet / Zoom with camera off or using your business persona name.
